PocketRounds Support

Need help with PocketRounds? Here's how to reach us, join a team, and manage your account.

Contact us

Email pocketrounds@ibsx.com. If something isn't working, tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and which iPhone and iOS version you're on. A screenshot helps a lot.

Joining a team with a seat code

Everyone starts as a Player. To join your team, you need a seat code from your Team Sponsor. It looks like ABC-1234.

  1. Ask your Team Sponsor for your seat code.
  2. In PocketRounds, open Account and tap Redeem a code.
  3. Type or paste the code, choose Player or Coach, and accept the invite.

Coaches get a coach seat code from their Team Sponsor and redeem it the same way.

Each code works once. If the app says a code was already used, ask your Team Sponsor for a new one.

Deleting your account

You can delete your PocketRounds account and the data tied to it right in the app:

  1. Open Account.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Can't get into the app? Email pocketrounds@ibsx.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. If you used Sign in with Apple, say so in your email. We'll confirm when it's done.

Practice data that's saved only on your device isn't stored with your account. It's removed when you delete the app.
