Privacy Policy

PocketRounds (iOS app)
Operator: Edward Nice / IBSX · Contact: cobb+pocketrounds@mail.grokbot.com
Effective date: July 18, 2026 · Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PocketRounds ("the App") handles information when you use our Bible Bowl practice, team, church, and event-hosting features on iOS, including TestFlight betas.

1. Overview

The App is designed to support Bible Bowl study and event operations. Much of your activity is stored on your device. Optional cloud features (when enabled) may sync limited data so teams and events can work across devices.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Display name, church or organization name, and role selections (e.g. player, coach, team sponsor, event host).
  • Team names, mottos/taglines, age brackets, logos or photos you upload, and seat/invite codes.
  • Event names, venues, addresses, contact details, documents, and registration information you enter as a host or sponsor.
  • Practice and competition-prep answers/scores generated while using the App.
  • Feedback you send (email, TestFlight feedback, or in-app feedback), which may include screenshots and device context you choose to attach.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data needed to run the App (e.g. on-device identifiers used for local storage, app version).
  • If cloud sync is enabled: timestamps and records required to synchronize profiles, teams, seats, events, and registrations.
  • Apple may collect diagnostic and usage data under Apple's policies when you use TestFlight or the App Store.

2.3 Purchases

In beta builds, purchase flows may be stubs for testing and may not charge a real payment method. If/when live In-App Purchases are enabled, payment processing is handled by Apple; we receive purchase status (e.g. product identifiers) as needed to unlock features, not your full payment card details.

3. How we use information

  • Provide practice, roster, team, church license, and event registration features.
  • Show event details, registered teams, documents, and related tools to authorized roles.
  • Sync data across devices when cloud features are turned on.
  • Respond to support and beta feedback.
  • Improve the App and fix bugs.
  • Comply with law and protect the security and integrity of the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We may share information only as follows:

  • With your organization / event participants: Information you enter for teams and events (e.g. team names, registrations, event contact fields) may be visible to other users according to role (host, sponsor, coach, player) as part of normal App functionality.
  • Service providers: Infrastructure providers that host optional cloud sync or email delivery, solely to operate the App (e.g. database/hosting). They process data under our instructions.
  • Apple: App Store, TestFlight, and Sign in with Apple / StoreKit as applicable under Apple's terms.
  • Legal: If required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Data storage and security

  • On-device: Core app data is stored in the App's sandbox on your iPhone/iPad (e.g. local database and files).
  • Cloud (optional): When configured, limited records may be stored on our cloud provider to enable multi-device use.
  • We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a small beta/production app. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Retention

On-device data remains until you delete it in the App (where offered), delete the App, or erase the device. Cloud records (if any) are retained while needed to provide the service or until you request deletion, unless a longer period is required by law. Beta feedback emails are retained as needed to support the beta.

7. Children's privacy

The App may be used in church and youth Bible Bowl settings. We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is needed for team seats, practice, and events as entered by organizers or the users themselves. Parents/guardians and church leaders should supervise youth accounts and what is entered into the App. Contact us to request deletion of a child's information.

8. Your choices

  • Edit or delete content you control in the App (teams, events, documents, etc., subject to role permissions).
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data from that device.
  • Email cobb+pocketrounds@mail.grokbot.com to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold in cloud systems or support mailboxes.
  • Leave the TestFlight beta at any time in the TestFlight app.

9. International users

The App is operated from the United States. If you use the App from another country, your information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our providers operate.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
